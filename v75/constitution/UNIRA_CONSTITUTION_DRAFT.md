# The Constitution of One Earth (UNIRA) — Draft 1

*Draft for discussion, 3 November 2026. Written for the author of UNIRA from the design rules, research results (V6.2–V7.4) and evidence reviews. Notes in italics give the evidence or reasoning behind an article; they are not part of the legal text.*

---

## Preamble

We, the people of the Earth, one humanity in many regions, cultures and languages, establish this Constitution:
- to end war;
- to share the Earth's resources fairly, by need and by the number of people in each place;
- to give every person a dignified and comfortable life and time to live it;
- to protect the planet for those who come after us.

**Fairness is the core of this Constitution.** No region shall rule another. No person or group shall own power over the common good. Every decision shall be based on data and science, made in the open, and answerable to the people. We produce what we need, for all regions.

---

## Part I — Foundations

**Article 1 — One Earth.** The Earth is one federation, called One Earth (UNIRA). Its members are regions: today's countries, or parts or unions of them, as their people decide.

**Article 2 — Equality of regions and people.**
1. Every region has equal dignity, and every person has equal rights.
2. No region, alliance of regions or institution may hold power over another region except through this Constitution.
3. There are no permanent seats, no vetoes, and no privileges of size, wealth or history.

**Article 3 — Fairness first, and four principles.** **Fairness is the core of every decision.** All public action is guided by:
- **Evidence:** decisions use data and science, published and open to challenge (Article 3a);
- **Equity:** resources go first to unmet needs;
- **Justice:** rights are equal and enforceable;
- **Sharing:** what the Earth provides belongs to all, and its use is accounted for openly.

**Article 3a — Data and science in every decision.**
1. Every decision of One Earth and of the regions on production, allocation, prices, salaries, the money cap, the environment and public services is based on measured data and the best available science.
2. The data, the methods and the reasons for each decision are published, with their uncertainty, so that anyone can check them.
3. An independent **Scientific Council**, drawn from all regions and all relevant sciences, reviews the methods used. Its opinions are public. A decision that departs from its advice must say why.
4. When new evidence shows that a decision or rule is wrong, it is reviewed. Rules carry their own tests of success and failure (as in Article 39).
5. **We produce what we need, for all regions:**
    - production is planned from measured needs and population in every region, plus the studied reserves of Article 11;
    - no more is produced than is needed, and no region is left without what it needs.

**Article 4 — Subsidiarity and diversity.**
1. Decisions are taken as close to the people as possible.
2. Each region governs itself according to its own religion, culture, language and traditions:
    - education;
    - local planning;
    - public holidays;
    - daily life.
3. One Earth decides only what must be decided together:
    - shared resources;
    - allocation between regions;
    - the currency;
    - rights;
    - the environment;
    - peace.

**Article 4a — Culture, belief and the individual.**
1. A region may give a religion, culture or language an official or traditional place in its public life.
2. Every person keeps freedom of thought, conscience and belief:
    - no one may be forced into a religion or belief;
    - no one may be punished for their religion or belief;
    - no one may be punished for changing their religion or belief, or for having none.
3. Minorities in every region are protected in their language, culture and worship.

**Article 4b — Borders and movement.**
1. **Each region controls its own borders.** It decides who may enter, stay, work and settle, by its own visa and residence rules, applied equally and without discrimination by origin, religion or ethnicity.
2. Every person may leave any region, including their own, and return to their own region.
3. **Protection in emergencies.** A person whose life is threatened by persecution or disaster is protected:
    - first in their own region, through rebuilding and needs-first allocation by One Earth (Article 10);
    - where that is impossible, other regions provide temporary shelter, shared among regions by their means and population through the One Earth Fund;
    - people return home when it is safe again.

*Evidence (`v76/migration/`): all 117.8 million people forcibly displaced at end-2025 fled persecution, conflict or violence, which this Constitution abolishes; 14.7 million returned home in 2025. Disasters displaced 45.8 million people in 2024, 99% weather-related, almost all inside their own country, which is why protection starts in the person's own region. Economic migration (281 million migrants in 2020) falls as living standards converge but does not vanish, and may rise temporarily while poorer regions catch up; regional visa rules manage it.*

**Article 5 — Peace.**
1. War is abolished.
2. No region may maintain armed forces or weapons of war, or produce them. **All arms belong to One Earth.**
3. Existing armed forces are dissolved during the transition. Their members are offered work, training and full income, including in health, construction and civil protection, or service in the One Earth Peace Force.
4. Disputes between regions are settled by the Court of One Earth (Article 32), whose rulings bind.
5. Weapons of mass destruction (nuclear, chemical, biological) are destroyed under independent verification and never rebuilt.

*Evidence: military spending is 2.5% of world output (SIPRI 2025), military activity about 5.5% of global emissions (SGR/CEOBS 2022), and armed forces number 27.4 million people (World Bank). UN peacekeeping uses about 59,000 uniformed personnel (peak 106,595 in 2015) on USD 5.6 bn a year, and peacekeepers are found to reduce violence against civilians. A Peace Force of about 500,000 frees about 26.9 million people and 2.45% of world output (`v76/peace_force/`). New armed forces are most at risk of coups while being built (De Bruin 2018), hence point 5 of Article 5a.*

**Article 5a — The One Earth Peace Force: the only armed force, under strict control.** One Earth holds the only armed force so that the Court's rulings and people's rights can be enforced everywhere. Because whoever controls the only armed force could dominate everyone, it is bound by these rules:
1. **Purpose:**
    - enforcing rulings of the Court of One Earth;
    - protecting people whose Article 6 rights are under attack;
    - responding to disasters together with the Protection Service.

   It is never used against peaceful protest, a lawful election or a region's democratic institutions.
2. **Deployment:** the Coordination Council may deploy the Force only with:
    - a ruling of the Court;
    - approval by double majority of the Council of Regions (Article 25).

   In an immediate threat to life it may act for at most 72 hours, then needs both.
3. **No region controls it:**
    - members come from all regions, with no region providing more than 5% of personnel or commanders;
    - commanders rotate every 3 years;
    - members do not serve in their own region.
4. **Limited size:**
    - the Force may never exceed **1,000,000 members** (about 12 per 100,000 people);
    - its budget may never exceed **0.15% of world output**;
    - within these ceilings its size is fixed by law (planned: about 500,000 members, about 0.05% of world output);
    - size, budget and every deployment are published each year in the Public Allocation Register.
5. **Build-up under watch:** the Force is built up gradually during the transition, with members from all regions from the first day and the Inspector-General in place before the first unit is formed.
6. **Independent oversight:** an Inspector-General of the Force, appointed by the Court, reports publicly. Every person harmed by the Force may bring a case before the Court.
7. **Regional police:** regions keep civilian police for everyday crime, trained to One Earth human-rights standards and equipped only for policing.

## Part II — Rights

**Article 6 — Guaranteed rights.** Every person has the right to:
- **food and clean water**, sufficient and secure, with access guaranteed;
- **a safe, comfortable home** of a decent size for their household, never seized and never subject to forced relocation;
- **health care and education**, free of charge, throughout life;
- **an income:**
    - by work, from the base salary of 5,000 U a month;
    - if unable to work for health reasons, a guaranteed 5,000 U;
    - in retirement, a needs-based income for life covering food, daily life and one holiday a year;
    - for children, the allowance of Article 15;
- **time:** a standard working week of 30 hours (6 hours × 5 days), so that families can live together;
- **energy, transport and digital access** sufficient for a comfortable life;
- **privacy, freedom of thought, expression, belief and association**;
- **movement within their own region, the right to leave any region and return to their own** (entry to other regions follows each region's visa rules, Article 4b);
- **take part in decisions**, to vote, to stand for office, to petition and to protest peacefully.

**Article 7 — Work.**
1. Every adult able to work has the right and the duty to contribute through work, with free choice of occupation.
2. Illness, disability, caring duties, study and retirement exempt a person from this duty without loss of income or rights.
3. No one may be forced into a particular job.

*This keeps the duty to work compatible with the international ban on forced labour (ILO Conventions 29 and 105).*

**Article 8 — Justice.**
1. Justice is independent, public and equal for all.
2. It aims first at prevention and repair, then at rehabilitation.
3. Punishment is proportionate and humane.
4. The death penalty, torture and collective punishment are forbidden.
5. Rights guaranteed in Article 6 are never withdrawn as a punishment.

*No constitution can guarantee a world without crime. This one removes its main causes (poverty, insecurity, inequality, war) and keeps fair justice for what remains.*

**Article 9 — Rights cannot be reduced.** No law, allocation decision, sanction against a region, or emergency may reduce the rights of Article 6 below their guaranteed level.

## Part III — The economy of One Earth

**Article 10 — Needs first, by population.** Each year the Allocation Authority (Article 26) directs new capacity to regions by measured needs and number of people, needs first, using a public formula. New capacity includes:
- energy systems;
- homes;
- health workers trained in-region;
- food;
- materials.

Surplus beyond a region's needs goes to the One Earth Fund for sharing.

*Evidence: needs-first allocation closes the world's energy, health and housing gaps within about 30 years; a market economy with the same resources does not (V6.8). The advantage survives realistic errors and leakage when safeguards are in place (V6.9).*

**Article 11 — Responsible production and stewardship.**
1. Before producing new, society maintains, repairs, renovates, reuses and converts what exists.
2. Nothing is produced only to sustain sales. There is no waste and no overproduction.
3. Whole-life environmental impacts are assessed.
4. Each region keeps studied, rotated reserves against catastrophes.

**Article 12 — One currency, the U.**
1. One Earth has one digital currency, the U, with no physical cash.
2. Every commercial transaction carries a verifiable invoice through the global transaction network. Personal gifts need none.
3. The system guarantees:
    - privacy;
    - access without a smartphone;
    - offline operation;
    - independent oversight;
    - the right to appeal.

**Article 13 — Fixed prices.**
1. Prices of essentials are fixed permanently.
2. They are set from the human work and resources each good embodies.
3. Gains in productivity become shorter working hours, never higher prices or unemployment.

**Article 14 — Salaries.**
1. The base salary is 5,000 U a month.
2. Salaries rise with experience, knowledge and the complexity of the work, up to 5 times the base (25,000 U).
3. The pay of enterprise owners and managers is a salary within the same limit.
4. Average pay may rise only as fast as productivity.

**Article 15 — Children.**
1. Each child brings an allowance of 1,000 U a month, limited so that adult household income plus child allowance does not exceed 15,000 U.
2. The disability guarantee counts as adult income for this limit but is never reduced by it.

**Article 16 — Enterprises.**
1. Enterprises are free to organise and innovate. There is no commercial competition for profit; ideas and methods may be compared openly.
2. Enterprises first pay for production, maintenance and investment.
3. Their surplus goes to the region, and the region's surplus to the One Earth Fund.

**Article 17 — Savings and the money cap.**
1. Savings are free and protected, up to a personal money cap set each year by a published coefficient, so that people keep working to produce what is needed.
2. Money above the cap, including large inheritances, goes to the One Earth Fund.
3. Homes are not money and are never taken.

*Evidence: without a cap, unspent money grows without limit at fixed prices; with it, money stays stable and about a fifth of a rich region's consumption value flows to the One Earth Fund each year (V6.4).*

**Article 17a — The One Earth Fund.**
1. The One Earth Fund receives:
    - money above the personal cap;
    - large inheritances above the cap;
    - regional surpluses beyond needs.
2. It pays for what One Earth allocates to every region:
    - new energy systems, homes and infrastructure;
    - the training of health workers and teachers;
    - the guarantees of Article 6 wherever a region's own means fall short;
    - the catastrophe reserves;
    - debts owed to outside creditors during the transition.
3. Everything the Fund receives and spends is published in the Public Allocation Register (Article 33).

*This is why money above the cap goes to One Earth: it returns, as resources, to the regions that need them.*

**Article 18 — Homes.**
1. Homes are priced between 100,000 and 500,000 U according to size and location.
2. An adult may own at most two homes, and a family at most two in total.
3. Zero-interest credit is available, repaid from salary.
4. A home inherited beyond the heir's first returns to the regional pool or is chosen by the heir.
5. Public housing is guaranteed for anyone without a salary.
6. New homes are built with low-carbon materials where possible.

**Article 19 — Metering.** Energy, travel and data are always metered and paid per unit, never at a flat rate.

*Evidence: free or flat-rate access raises use of goods without natural limits by 14–70%; a 20–30% energy rebound would break the climate limit (V7.1).*

**Article 20 — No public debt.** No region carries public debt.
1. Debts between members are cancelled.
2. Bonds held by savers and pension funds count as their protected savings.
3. Debts to outside creditors are paid on schedule until they join.

**Article 21 — Within Earth's limits.**
1. Total energy use, emissions, materials, land and water stay within limits set from the best science.
2. Regions using more than a comfortable level of energy reduce their use each year while regions below it rise.

*Evidence: 40 GJ of final energy per person for everyone fits a 1.5 °C pathway with regions above it reducing about 3–4% a year (V6.6, V6.7).*

## Part IV — Institutions: one strong Earth, no ruling region

**Article 22 — Separation of powers.** No person or body holds more than one of these powers:
- making law;
- carrying it out;
- allocating resources;
- measuring needs;
- auditing;
- judging.

**Article 23 — The Assembly of the Peoples.**
1. The Assembly has 1,000 members, elected every 5 years by direct, secret, universal vote.
2. Seats are shared among regions in proportion to the **square root of their population** (the Penrose rule, which gives every citizen equal voting power in a union of regions). Every region has at least 1 seat, and no region more than **5%** of all seats.

*Test on 2024 populations (`v75/constitution/voting.py`): the largest regions, India and China, each get about 4.4% of seats, against 16–17% under pure proportionality.*

**Article 24 — The Council of Regions.**
1. Each region sends one representative with one equal vote, whatever its size or wealth.

**Article 25 — How laws are made.**
1. A law passes with a majority in the Assembly of the Peoples and a **double majority** in the Council of Regions:
    - at least **55% of regions**;
    - representing at least **65% of the world's population, counted by the square root of each region's population**, as in Article 23.
2. Neither the largest regions nor the smallest can decide alone.

*Test on 2024 populations: passing a law needs at least 120 of 217 regions; blocking one needs at least 21 (`v75/outputs/voting.json`). Counting raw population instead would let India and China together (35.2% of humanity) block any law alone. The thresholds (55%/65%) follow the Council of the European Union and were agreed by the author on 2026-11-03.*

**Article 26 — The Allocation Authority.**
1. It applies the public allocation formula of Article 10 to the needs measured by the Statistical Office.
2. It has no power to favour any region.
3. Changing the formula requires a law under Article 25.

**Article 27 — The Coordination Council (executive).**
1. Seven to fifteen coordinators, from different regions, are elected by the Assembly for a single 5-year term.
2. The chair rotates every year.
3. No region may hold the chair twice in a row.

**Article 28 — The Statistical Office.** It measures needs in every region at least once a year with stated uncertainty, under public, uniform methods, and is independent of all other bodies (Article 37).

**Article 29 — The Audit Authority.** It audits every region every year (Article 36). Its budget is fixed by this Constitution as a share of allocated value.

**Article 30 — Terms and rotation.**
1. No person holds a One Earth office for more than 10 years in total.
2. All office-holders declare their assets publicly each year and are subject to the money cap.
3. No one may decide an allocation to a region where they or their family hold an interest.

**Article 31 — Regions.**
1. Each region has its own democratic government and parliament, elected by its people, with full powers under Article 4.
2. Regional governments cannot be dissolved by One Earth except by a ruling of the Court for a grave breach of rights.

**Article 32 — The Court of One Earth.**
1. Judges are appointed for one 12-year term by the Council of Regions from candidates proposed by an independent judicial commission, and are drawn from all continents.
2. The Court:
    - protects rights;
    - settles disputes between regions;
    - reviews laws and allocations against this Constitution.
3. Every person may bring a case.

## Part V — Safeguards

**Article 33 — Public Allocation Register.** Every allocation is published within 30 days in a free, open register stating what, where, how much, why and on which data. Personal data are never published.
*Evidence: when allocations were published in Uganda, leakage fell from 87% to under 20%.*

**Article 34 — Direct digital delivery and verification.**
1. Payments go directly, in U, to those who deliver.
2. Delivery is verified on site and by remote sensing before it is recorded as complete.
*Evidence: digital payment cut leakage by 19–24% in India.*

**Article 35 — Community monitoring and whistleblowers.**
1. Every community may monitor allocations and delivery.
2. People who report misuse are protected.
3. Every person may complain and receive a reasoned answer within 90 days.

**Article 36 — Independent audits.** Every region is audited every year, at random and without notice. All reports are public.
*Evidence: audits reduced missing funds by 8 percentage points (Indonesia) and corruption by 8–13% (Brazil).*

**Article 37 — Timely and honest data.**
1. Needs data are less than one year old.
2. Data are checked against independent sources.
3. Regions may not change methods alone.

**Article 38 — Correction without punishing people.** Misallocated resources are recovered and reallocated. A region found misreporting loses priority for discretionary allocations, never its people's rights.

**Article 39 — Built-in test of the system.**
1. World leakage and data age are published each year.
2. If leakage exceeds 25% or data are more than 2 years old, the Assembly must review the allocation system within a year.
3. If leakage exceeds 45% or data are more than 5 years old for 3 consecutive years, the allocation system must be reformed by law.
*Evidence: above those levels, needs-first allocation loses its measured advantage (V6.9).*

## Part VI — Joining, leaving and changing this Constitution

**Article 40 — Joining.**
1. A region joins by referendum of its people.
2. It passes through three stages:
    - observer, building the safeguard institutions;
    - associate, receiving needs-based transfers and adopting the 30-hour week;
    - full member, adopting the U and all rules.

**Article 41 — Leaving.** A region may leave by referendum, with a negotiated settlement. Every person may leave any region at any time; entry elsewhere follows Article 4b.

**Article 42 — Amendments.**
1. This Constitution is amended by a two-thirds majority in the Assembly of the Peoples, a double majority of 65% of regions representing 65% of the population in the Council of Regions, and a world referendum.
2. Article 6 rights and Article 9 may never be reduced by amendment.

## Transitional provisions

**T-1. Conversion.**
1. Wages move directly onto the UNIRA scale at full membership. Prices of essentials are set from embodied work. There is no single political exchange rate.
2. Savings convert at local basket parity.
3. The money cap phases in over 10 years. Money above it goes to the One Earth Fund. The cap applies to wealth held on the day of the joining vote.

**T-2. Homes and mortgages.**
1. Homes are kept.
2. Homes beyond two may be sold to the regional pool within 10 years.
3. Mortgages become zero-interest U credit.

**T-3. Enterprises.** Owners receive their share in U under the cap. Old property claims are settled by compensation, never by giving property back.

**T-4. Debts.**
1. Members' public debts are cancelled.
2. Bonds held by savers and pension funds count as their savings.
3. Outside creditors are paid on a schedule.

**T-5. Disarmament.** Armed forces are dissolved within 5 years of full membership, and every member is offered work, training and full income.

**T-6. Protecting production.** New members keep their productive capacity running through transitional support from the union.

### Notes for the author (not part of the text)
- **Representation (agreed 2026-11-03).** 55%/65% double majority and a maximum seat share per region. The square-root weighting is added so that no pair of regions can block the world.
- **Crime and stress.** The evidence links violence and stress to poverty, insecurity, inequality and war, which this Constitution removes or reduces. It does not support a claim that crime disappears, so independent justice is kept (Articles 8, 32).
- **What still needs research.**
    - The exact allocation formula (Article 10).
    - The size of the Protection Service.
    - The Peace Force ceiling (1,000,000 members, 0.15% of world output) rests on UN peacekeeping and disaster-response evidence and two assumptions (a 3× allowance for rotation, training and reserve; USD 100–150k per member).
    - How the money cap is set each year (Article 17).
