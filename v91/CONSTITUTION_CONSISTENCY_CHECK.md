# V9.1 — consistency check of the three approved constitutional changes

**Decision (author, 2026-11-05):** the three V9.1 proposals are approved. Before incorporation they were checked against the articles they touch. Four small clarifications were added in drafting; they are listed here so the author can reverse any of them.

## The changes, as incorporated in `v75/constitution/UNIRA_CONSTITUTION_DRAFT.md`

- **Article 4c — No permanent exclusion** (after Article 4b): no community permanently excluded from power or office within its region; each region chooses how to include its communities; no compulsory declaration of community; after ten years without a share of power a community may ask the Court to review; the Court may order a proposed remedy, never impose one.
- **Article 10f.6 — After a conflict ends:** ten years of priority support for a lasting peace, at the region's request and with its participation.
- **T-9 — Institutional reform at a safe pace:** staged institutional changes where institutions are weak, never delaying services, rights or safeguards.

## Check against the existing articles

| Existing protection | Risk from the new text | How it is handled | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Art. 4, 31: subsidiarity and regional self-government | One Earth imposing a model of inclusion | 4c.2 leaves the model to the region; 4c.4 lets the Court order a proposal, never a remedy, and refers to 31.2 for dissolution | Consistent |
| Art. 4a.1: a region may give a religion, culture or language an official place | An official place could be used to exclude other communities from office | **Clarification added to 4c.1:** an official place never excludes any community from power or office | Consistent after clarification |
| Art. 4a.2–3: freedom of belief, protected minorities | Compulsory community labels | 4c.3: no one is required to declare a community; participation as an individual is preserved | Consistent; strengthens 4a.3 |
| Art. 6, 12a, 3c.4: privacy, no files, no scoring | A review of exclusion needing data on persons | **Clarification added to 4c.4:** the Court relies on public records and the community's own account, never files on persons | Consistent after clarification |
| Art. 26a.2: allocation depends only on needs and population | "Priority support" after a conflict read as a favour | **Clarification added to 10f.6:** support follows the needs-based formula of Articles 10 and 26a; conflict raises measured needs, it is no exception | Consistent after clarification |
| Art. 10e, 10f.2: essential services first, never withheld | Post-conflict support delaying other regions' services | 10f.6 sits inside 10e and 10f.2 and uses the same formula | Consistent |
| Art. 5a–5c: Peace Force rules | Post-conflict support becoming an occupation | 10f.6: any presence of the Force stays governed by 5a–5c; support is at the region's request | Consistent |
| Art. 8, 4b.3: justice and repair; return home when safe | — | 10f.6 refers to both | Consistent |
| Art. 9, 9a: rights not reducible; emergency limits | T-9 used to postpone rights or elections | **Clarification added to T-9.2:** pacing never delays Article 6 rights, essential services, the safeguards of T-7, elections on fixed dates or the protected core | Consistent after clarification |
| T-7: safeguards before power | T-9 staging could defer the safeguards | Same clarification: T-7 safeguards are expressly outside pacing | Consistent |
| Art. 22a, 39a: separation of powers; Democracy Monitor | A government using "pacing" to avoid scrutiny | **Clarification added to T-9.1:** the staging plan is approved by the region's parliament and published with end dates; T-9.3 lets any person or community ask the Court whether pacing is being used to delay | Consistent after clarification |
| Art. 42a: protected core | — | Nothing in the core is weakened. Article 4c and T-9 are **not** added to the core; that is for the author to decide | No change |

## Open points

- **Whether 4c should join the protected core (42a).** Not done without the author's decision. Adding it would make the ban on permanent exclusion unamendable.
- **"A share in regional power" (4c.4):** the threshold and evidence for the Court's review are left to law and to the Court's practice.
- **How effective these provisions are is not established.** The evidence behind them is associations from published studies and a simulation (see the research notes in Articles 4c, 10f and T-9); under Article 10e.6 their effect is to be monitored and the provisions adjusted.

**Conclusion:** no inconsistency was found after the four clarifications.
