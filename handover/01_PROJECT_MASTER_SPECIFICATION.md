# UNIRA — MASTER SPECIFICATION AND SHARED DESIGN DECISIONS

## Vision
UNIRA is a proposed worldwide cooperative, needs-based economy and democratic global federation. The research hypothesis is that international cooperation, better resource allocation, reduced avoidable waste and responsible technology could support a **universally dignified, comfortable standard of living**, shorter paid workweeks and ecological sustainability. Peaceful coexistence, rights, local autonomy, cultural diversity, transparency and accountability are essential. These are **normative objectives**, not demonstrated outcomes.

## Institutional and constitutional design
- One coordinated worldwide economic framework with democratic global federation and meaningful regional/local autonomy. Subsidiarity and individual/cultural rights must be protected. No coercive forced relocation, seizure of homes or suppression of political dissent.
- Resources shared multilaterally based on needs, availability, logistics and ecological limits rather than one-for-one bilateral barter. Peaceful conflict resolution, disarmament and reduced resource conflict are goals, not guaranteed predictions.
- Public-interest production coordinated with decentralised enterprises, innovation and constructive competition of ideas. Enterprises pay genuine production, maintenance and investment requirements first; only **distributable** surplus goes to the public fund. Do not double-count that surplus or presume it is sufficient to finance public obligations.
- **Article 11 — Responsible Production and Resource Stewardship:** determine actual needs and existing usable stock; maintain/repair/renovate/reuse/redistribute/convert before producing or building new, where appropriate. Stock-first is a decision priority, NOT an absolute ban on new production. Assess whole-life impacts and preserve comfort, safety and rights. Food and manufactured goods must not be overproduced merely to sustain sales.
- **Article 12 — Universal Digital Transaction Integrity:** one fully digital UNIRA currency (U), no physical cash, and a global transaction-validation/billing infrastructure (UGTN). Commercial purchases require licensed counterparties and verifiable invoices; legitimate personal gifts/transfers need no commercial invoice. Build strong privacy, access without smartphones, offline resilience, independent oversight, appeal rights and protections against surveillance/financial exclusion. Cashlessness cannot eliminate crime, illegal markets or addiction.

## Social guarantees and compensation — agreed rules to model, not yet validated
- Standard paid work for adults able to work: **6 hours/day × 5 days/week = 30 hours/week**. Annual effective hours depend on leave, holidays, sickness, part-time and workforce status; never automatically assume 52 working weeks.
- Base adult salary: **5,000 U/month**. Individual salary ceiling: **25,000 U/month**. Exact compensation gradients, essential-service staffing incentives and treatment of self-employment remain to be designed.
- Adult unable to work for medical reasons receives a **5,000 U/month income guarantee**; this guarantee must not be reduced by the child-benefit means test. Universal essential healthcare and education are separately guaranteed, not charged against the adult's nominal salary.
- Child allowance **1,000 U/month per child**, constrained by household adult income plus child allowance ≤ **15,000 U/month**. Formula: `child_benefit = min(1000 * number_of_children, max(0, 15000 - adult_household_income))`. The 15,000 U threshold is **NOT a ceiling on adult household earnings**. Disability guarantee counts toward adult household income for the child-benefit calculation, but remains intact.
- One U is NOT defined as one euro or dollar. A salary amount is meaningless as a welfare claim without a specified, feasible consumption basket and stable purchasing power.

## Living standards
- Safe, appropriately sized, comfortable homes; adequate privacy, accessibility, energy performance, local amenities and household choice. Not tiny compulsory accommodation, not unrestricted luxury mansions. Quality standards must vary sensibly with family size and needs.
- Reliable food, clean water, energy, transport, healthcare, education, childcare, eldercare, disability support and meaningful leisure. Define and measure service quality, availability and environmental sustainability.
- Stock-first housing: evaluate vacancy status, legal availability, consent, structural condition, renovation cost, spatial matching, household size and preferences; never count every vacant home as automatically recoverable.
- Technology and automation should free human time, not be assumed to deliver magical productivity increases.

## Economic scientific discipline
- Keep **physical feasibility** (labour hours, materials, energy, capital, land, time, emissions) distinct from **financial consistency** (deposits, salaries, public funds, credit, balance sheets, purchasing power).
- Compare three matched-resource counterfactuals: (A) current economy, (B) realistic reformed current economy, (C) UNIRA. Identical initial productive endowments, demographics, needs and exogenous shocks. No arbitrary UNIRA efficiency advantage.
- Use verified empirical data wherever possible; explicitly tag synthetic assumptions and scenario choices. Cost of today's economy is NOT UNIRA's real resource cost; record both contemporary market-price benchmarks and physical input requirements.
- Environmental lifecycle impacts and opportunity costs matter even if monetary profit or land speculation are removed. Resource scarcity and hard trade-offs remain.
- Report unmet needs, rights violations, shortages, emissions, finance gaps, workforce bottlenecks and sensitivity intervals. Never claim feasibility from an artificially feasible baseline.

## Project deliverables and ambition
A reproducible, auditable Python economic simulation; empirically calibrated comparative studies; research working paper(s); constitutional/governance and implementation framework; eventual book **UNIRA: One Humanity, One Future**. The research should be falsifiable and invite independent critique. The user prefers substantive progress without repeated permission requests.
