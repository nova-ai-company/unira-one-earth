# OPEN DESIGN DECISIONS, RESEARCH RISKS AND ADVERSARIAL QUESTIONS

## Unresolved design questions
- Currency issuance, public fund replenishment, deposit banking, investment finance, lending/repayment/defaults, monetary-policy governance and U purchasing power.
- How scarce luxury goods, desirable locations, rare medical services and finite energy are allocated without arbitrary privilege, black markets or surveillance.
- Exact salary progression 5,000–25,000 U, essential-work incentives, part-time rules, student/retiree entitlements, disability assessment with rights and appeal, informal/unpaid care.
- Child benefit cliff/means-test design: formula is agreed but may create marginal incentives and inequities; evaluate without silently changing rule.
- Rights of property owners and tenants, voluntary relocation, collective land use, democratic allocation, anti-corruption and independent courts.
- Public/private/cooperative firm structure, price signals vs planning, firm failure, innovation rewards, quality control and entrepreneurship.
- Migration, cross-border transfers, environmental justice, governance at world/regional/local levels, democratic exit/constitutional amendment procedures.
- Fully digital currency privacy: offline access, cashless exclusion, resilience to cyberattacks and disasters, proportional fraud controls, due process and oversight.
- What counts as a comfortable standard of living across climates, cultures, family sizes, disability and preference variation.

## Critical falsification tests
1. 30h labour constraint: can healthcare, eldercare, education and utilities maintain service coverage with training/shift requirements?
2. Inflation/shortages: does 5,000 U guarantee exceed sustainable physical consumption capacity under shocks?
3. Investment: can system fund replacement, transition and innovation while meeting current needs and ecological limits?
4. Environmental rebound: do efficiency gains induce higher consumption elsewhere?
5. Coordination: can distributed information be aggregated without unmanageable errors, latency or political capture?
6. Scarcity and freedom: do allocation rules violate autonomy or create hidden privilege?
7. Climate and geopolitics: what happens with crop failure, energy shortages, supply chain disruption and hostile actors?
8. Compared to a well-designed reform baseline, are improvements attributable to UNIRA's distinctive institutions rather than assumptions?
9. Global equity: who bears material extraction, waste and transition costs?
10. Sensitivity: do outcomes collapse when productivity/consent/renovation rates are less favourable?

## Scientific status as of 2026-11-03
Many runnable synthetic prototypes, some source-linked observed indicators, and a French electricity/labour anchor. NO verified global empirical calibration, no demonstrated 30h feasibility across sectors, no validated UNIRA U purchasing power, no independently reviewed welfare/environmental advantage. Treat all policy claims as hypotheses pending empirical testing.
