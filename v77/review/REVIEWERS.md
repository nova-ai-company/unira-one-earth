# Reviewers needed for the UNIRA research

**Status.** No one listed here has been contacted. Named researchers are *suggestions only*: their published work is cited in the UNIRA evidence files, so they are well placed to judge whether it has been used correctly. Being named does not imply any involvement or endorsement. This document gives no contact details. Reach people through their institutional pages or through journal editors.

**General principles for choosing reviewers**

- **Two per field where possible.** One reviewer should be sympathetic to post-growth or cooperative-economy research, and one sceptical, for example from mainstream labour, public-finance or growth economics.
- **Avoid reviewers whose own estimates drive a headline result** as the *only* reviewer in that field. Their work is used in the research, so they are reviewing its use of their own numbers. Pair them with someone independent.
- **Ask for a declaration of interests,** including political affiliation with world-federalist or degrowth movements, or with organisations opposed to them.
- **Ask that reviews be signed or anonymous, as the reviewer prefers,** and say in advance that they will be published alongside the responses.

## Suggested routes for collective review

- **Working-paper series and preprint servers** with open comment, e.g. SSRN, RePEc, or OSF Preprints. These would host `v67/WORKING_PAPER_02.md` together with its replication package.
- **Conference sessions** of ecological-economics societies, Degrowth / post-growth conferences, and labour-economics and development-economics meetings. Mainstream sessions are needed alongside these, to get sceptical review.
- **A structured red-team panel** of 6–10 reviewers, one per field above, each using `FEEDBACK_FORM.md`. Responses would be published in a `v77/review/responses/` folder.
- **A replication request** to a replication institute or journal data editor, for the reproducibility claims alone.
