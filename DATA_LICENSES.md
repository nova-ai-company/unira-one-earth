# Data sources and licences

The frozen data snapshots in this repository (`*/data/raw/`) are redistributed under their original terms. Each file is listed with its source URL, retrieval time, SHA-256 hash and licence in the `raw_manifest.csv` next to it.

| Source | Licence | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| Eurostat (national accounts, Labour Force Survey, health, housing, income) | CC BY 4.0 (Commission Decision 2011/833/EU) | v62, v63, v64, v66, v74 |
| World Bank Open Data (WDI, population projections, armed forces) | CC BY 4.0 | v65, v66 |
| ILOSTAT (ILO modelled estimates, hours) | CC BY 4.0 | v65 |
| FAOSTAT Food Balance Sheets | CC BY 4.0 (FAO Statistical Database Terms of Use) | v65 |
| Our World in Data (energy) | CC BY 4.0; underlying Energy Institute, Ember and EIA terms apply | v65 |
| OECD Health Statistics | CC BY 4.0 (OECD Terms and Conditions) | v66 |
| UN DESA World Population Prospects 2024 | CC BY 3.0 IGO | v66 |
| UN Statistics Division M49 codes | UN terms of use | v65, v66 |
| **WHO Global Health Observatory (health workforce)** | **CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO: non-commercial use only, share alike** | v65, v66 |
| Deetman et al. (2020) BUMA model inputs | MIT | v66 |
| IAMC common-definitions | CC0 1.0 | v66 |

Published reports and articles cited as evidence (IPCC, IEA, Lancet, WHO, ILO, national statistics and others) are **not** redistributed. Only their bibliographic details, short quoted figures and links are kept, in the `evidence/` files and `REFERENCES.md`.
