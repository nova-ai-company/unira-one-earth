## Known caveats

- Food (FAO FBS): 11 economies (incl. Japan, Sudan, Mali, Chad, Somalia) are absent from the current FAOSTAT FBS bulk release (2010-2023); their values come from OWID's republication of an earlier FAO release (year 2022) and are flagged REPORTED_NEEDS_RECHECK. Values are dietary energy *supply* (availability), not consumption; Taiwan mapped via ISO numeric 158 (absent from the UNSD M49 page); FAO 'China' (M49 159) aggregate is excluded, 'China, mainland' maps to CHN, Hong Kong/Macao separately.
- Energy (OWID): REPORTED_NEEDS_RECHECK. Primary energy uses OWID's substitution method (non-fossil electricity grossed up to fossil-equivalent input); it is *primary*, not final, energy. Small economies come from EIA with older years. Recheck against Energy Institute Statistical Review / IEA before use in final results.
- Health workforce (WHO NHWA): reporting years vary widely; WORLD row is WHO's period estimate (2016-2024). Values older than 2015 are excluded.
- Labour (ILO modelled estimates): employment and labour force are model-based for countries without recent surveys. Hours actually worked are survey-based only and patchy; missing countries (incl. China) are left MISSING, not imputed. The hours WORLD row is ILO's published world aggregate (ref_area X01), which is model-based.
- The GHO OData endpoint ghoapi.azureedge.net returned HTTP 500 on 2026-11-03; WHO data were taken from WHO's public xMart OData (GHO mart, FACT view), the backend of the same GHO data.
- No monetary indicators are included (UNIRA works in hours and physical units).
