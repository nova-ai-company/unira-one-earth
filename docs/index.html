UNIRA — One Humanity, One Future

A proposal for one world federation of equal regions, with a 30-hour week, fixed prices, allocation by need and no war, tested against real data. Everything here is open: the data, the code, the results and their limits. Status: preliminary research, made with an AI assistant, not yet peer reviewed (see the self-audit under "For reviewers").

To discuss, report an error or suggest better data, open an issue or a discussion in the project repository.
