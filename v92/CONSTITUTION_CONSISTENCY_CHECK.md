# Consistency check of the constitution changes approved on 8 November 2026

**Decision (author, 2026-11-08):** three approvals. (1) The V9.2 text in `PROPOSED_CONSTITUTION_CHANGES.md`: Article 6 "time", new T-10, Article 40.2. (2) T-8.5: in health and education, faculties and trainers come before new posts. (3) T-5: the first members keep their defence until the federation can protect them. For (1) the author approved a written text. For (2) and (3) the author approved the principle; the text was drafted on incorporation, and every drafting choice is listed below so that the author can reverse it.

## The changes, as incorporated in `v75/constitution/UNIRA_CONSTITUTION_DRAFT.md`

- **Article 6, "time":** a standard week of 30 hours, reached in each region as set out in T-10; from the first day no one is required to work more than 40 hours a week, or more than before their region joined.
- **T-10 — The working week during the transition** (after T-9): the starting week is the shortest whole-hour week the region's existing labour reserve can cover without loss of output, measured by the body of T-8.6, never above 40 hours or the pre-joining week; the full base salary from the first day; the week shortened each year by measured productivity until 30 hours, gains to time first and only then to Article 14c; never longer again except in a declared emergency; the reserve brought into work before any paper reduction; all published.
- **Article 40.2:** the associate stage adopts "the working week of Article 6 and T-10".
- **T-8.5:** one sentence added: in health and education, faculties and trainers come before new posts, because the capacity to train, not the length of courses, sets the date on which care for all is reached.
- **T-5 — Disarmament**, rewritten: dissolution within five years of the day the Court finds, on the Council's published assessment, that the region's security no longer depends on its own forces (the federation at a size and the Peace Force at a strength set by law, or every neighbour a member or bound by a verified agreement); until then the existing forces serve defence only, under limits; the Peace Force takes over as the region disarms; review every two years.

## Drafting choices made on incorporation (reversible)

1. **T-10.1:** the research values ("33–40 hours in Europe, 35 in France") were moved from the legal text into the italic note, as the proposal itself recommended that no starting hour stand in the constitution.
2. **T-8.5:** the phrase is placed in "health and education" and worded "come before new posts", so that it ranks investments without promising a date.
3. **T-5.2, two triggers:** a size-and-strength threshold set by law (T-8.9 style), *or* all neighbours members or bound by a verified non-aggression and security agreement. Either suffices. The Court decides on the Council's assessment, mirroring its role in Article 5.4.
4. **T-5.3, the limits** on forces kept during the transition: defence of the region only; never inside the federation or against citizens, elections, courts or a vote to leave; no growth beyond the strength on the joining day; arms production limited to maintenance, exports stopped; the Inspector-General's access; yearly publication in the Register; civilian democratic authority; no new armed body. These were drafted from Articles 5a.1, 5a.4, 5a.6 and 5c so that regional forces obey the same rules as the Peace Force.
5. **T-5.5:** review at least every two years; any member may ask the Court to find that its conditions are met or no longer met.
6. **Weapons of mass destruction:** T-5 says nothing about them, so Article 5.5 applies as written (destroyed under verification during the transition). Whether destruction should wait for the same conditions is **left open for the author**; it is a large question for a nuclear-armed first member and was not decided here.

## The V6.4 check at 35 hours

The lifecycle money model (`v64/run_lifecycle.py`) was rerun with the week set to 35 hours instead of 30, for productivity growth of 0, 1 and 2% a year and the same seed. Every output except the reported weekly hours is **identical**: the model is in U and in consumption, and weekly hours enter only as the scalar from which the falling week is reported. The money-cap results of V6.4 and V7.9 therefore do not depend on the starting week. The model's hours rule (all gains to time) has no 30-hour floor and no switch to Article 14c, so its hours series after 30 hours is illustrative only. The check was run ad hoc and is not part of the pipeline; it changes no published output.

## Open points for the author

1. **Reading of the Article 6 right.** The draft treats 30 hours as the standard and T-10 as the path. If the author prefers, Article 6 could say "a standard working week falling to 30 hours" to make the transitional reading explicit.
2. **The thresholds of T-5.2** (federation size, Peace Force strength) have not been studied by this project. They are recorded as a research item in the constitution's notes and in review question 13.
3. **Weapons of mass destruction during the transition** (drafting choice 6).
4. **Protected core:** whether Article 4c and T-9 (V9.1) join Article 42a is still undecided.
5. **Not drafted:** the proposal that every economic chapter be checked for a single member trading with a market world (D + Q + C, the Trade Office of 12b) remains a research item, not a constitutional change.
6. **The book draft** (`v73/book/`) still describes a 30-hour week from the first day and disarmament within five years of membership. It is a second draft dated 3 November 2026 and is to be revised in the next draft, not patched.

**Conclusion:** no inconsistency was found. One article in the protected core (5b) was deliberately left untouched; one question (weapons of mass destruction) was deliberately left open.
