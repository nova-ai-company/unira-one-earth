## Known caveats

- Armed forces (WB MS.MIL.TOTL.P1, from IISS Military Balance): active + paramilitary forces; reservists excluded. Latest WB year is often 2020; a few countries have no data.
- Graduates: WHO NHWA module 2 (education) graduate counts are only exposed through the NHWA portal's embedded Power BI dashboard (apps.who.int/nhwaportal); they are absent from the GHO / xMart public OData registry (searched 2026-11-03: no 'graduat' HWF indicator). Graduate numbers therefore come from OECD Health Statistics (OECD + partner countries incl. IDN, THA, ARG, COL, UKR...); large populations (CHN, IND, NGA, PAK, BRA...) are MISSING. Eurostat hlth_rs_grd2 counts EU-recognised qualifications (Directive 2005/36/EC) and can differ from OECD (e.g. associate nurses excluded).
- OECD MINU = professional + associate professional nurses; midwives (MIDW) are separate. Values with OECD status flags (break, estimate, provisional) are kept as published.
- Stocks at graduate year: only where WHO HWF has exactly that year; otherwise absent.
- Floor area: no consolidated official country dataset exists (checked OECD AHD HC2.1 = rooms per person only; OWID has no series; Odyssee-Mure needs a login). The global series is MODELLED: IMAGE (SSP2) regional residential floor space per capita as used in Deetman et al. 2020 (J. Clean. Prod., doi:10.1016/j.jclepro.2019.118658), urban/rural combined with IMAGE rural population share. 2020 is a model year (IMAGE calibrated on historical data to ~2010). Region numbering 1-26 follows the IMAGE convention (PBL region page was down on 2026-11-03); it is verified in tests by matching BUMA region populations against World Bank country populations. Country->region list from IAMC common-definitions IMAGE 3.2 (some territories, e.g. South Sudan, Rep. Congo, Kosovo, are not listed there).
- Eurostat floor area per person is derived (average dwelling size / average household size, 2012); it approximates m2 per person but weights by dwelling, not person.
- Projections: population 2050 from UN WPP 2024 medium (thousands, as published) and from the World Bank Population Estimates and Projections database (source 40, its own projection; differs slightly from WPP). Old-age dependency 2050 and 65+ share 2050 come from WB source 40 (the WPP demographic-indicator file has no dependency ratio).
- The UN DESA Data Portal API requires a bearer token; the WPP 2024 bulk CSV was used instead.
- No monetary indicators are included (UNIRA works in hours and physical units).
