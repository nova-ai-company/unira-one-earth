# UNIRA v66 ecology: is a comfortable life for 9.7 bn compatible with ecological limits?

Date: 2026-11-03. Sources and exact quoted values: `evidence.csv`. Machine-readable constraints: `constraints.json`.
"Verified" means the number was read in fetched source text. Derived values are marked as derived. The 2050 population is 9.66 bn (UN WPP 2024 medium; not re-fetched).

## 1. Carbon budget (the binding constraint)
| Budget (GtCO2) | 1.5C 50% | 1.5C 67% | 2C 50% | 2C 67% | base |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IPCC AR6 WG1 Table SPM.2 | 500 | 400 | 1350 | 1150 | from 1 Jan 2020 |
| Lamboll et al. 2023 | 250 | — | 1200 | — | from Jan 2023 |
| Forster et al. 2025 (IGCC 2024) | **130** | **80** | **1050** | **870** | from start 2025 |
| Global Carbon Budget 2025 | 170 | — | — | — | from start 2026 |

Total CO2 in 2025 is 42.2 Gt (GCB 2025). The 1.5C 50% budget is therefore about 3–4 years of current emissions. Human-induced warming was 1.36 C in 2024 and is rising 0.27 C/decade. **No energy level now avoids at least a temporary overshoot of 1.5C.** 2C (67%) is still open: a linear decline from 2025 reaches net zero around 2066.

## 2. Energy
| 2050 final energy | EJ | GJ/cap (9.66 bn) |
|---|---|---|
| Decent living (Millward-Hopkins 2020) | 149 | 15.4 (paper: 15.3) |
| LED (Grubler 2018) | 245 | 25.4 |
| IEA NZE 2023 | 343 | 35.2 |
| **UNIRA 40 GJ for all** | **386** | **40** |
| AR6 C1 median (p5–p95) | 410 (325–540) | 42.4 (33.6–55.9) |
| AR6 C3 (2C >67%) median | 448 (344–540) | 46.4 |
| Today (2022, IEA) | 442 | ~55 |

Millward-Hopkins 2020 also finds that 40 GJ/cap (HD-LAT) gives "just under 400 EJ", equal to the IEA SDS. This independently matches the 386 EJ figure.

**Build-out.** Additions in 2025 were 692 GW of renewables in total, including 510 GW solar and 159 GW wind (IRENA 2026), a record. Tripling to 11.2 TW by 2030 needs 1,122 GW/yr (IRENA, Oct 2025). NZE needs 820 GW PV plus 320 GW wind, about 1.14 TW/yr by 2030, with electricity providing 53% of final energy in 2050. Scaled to 386 EJ, that is about 1.3 TW/yr, or 1.9× the 2025 record (derived, first-order).

## 3. Food and land
- Cropland was 1,580 Mha (0.2 ha/cap) and agricultural land 4,788 Mha in 2021 (FAO).
- EAT-Lancet 2025 uses 2,400 kcal (down from 2,500). Its land rule is no conversion of intact land, with agricultural land kept below 48 M km². Other limits: nitrogen surplus below 57 Tg/yr, blue water about 2,000 km³/yr, and food-system GHG 5 GtCO2e by 2050. Its finding is a "healthy diet for 9.6 billion people" if diets shift (ruminant meat −33%), food loss and waste is halved, and productivity rises 7–10%. In that scenario cropland changes by +11% (−3 to +42%) while total agricultural land changes by −7%.
- Losses: 13.3% is lost between harvest and retail (FAO 2023). 19% (1.05 bn t, 132 kg/cap) is wasted at retail, food service and households (UNEP FWI 2024). Together this is about 775 kcal/cap/day, roughly 20% of agricultural land (EAT-Lancet 2025).
- **Verdict:** 2,470 kcal for 9.7 bn fits only with a planetary-health diet composition and waste halved. The UNIRA rule 13 halving is necessary but not sufficient: diet composition is the binding lever for land and nitrogen.

## 4. Materials
Extraction rose from 30 to 106 Gt/yr, or 23 to 39 kg/person/day (UNEP IRP GRO 2024), which is about 14.2 t/cap/yr. The sustainable range is 3–8 t/cap: O'Neill 2018 uses 7.2 t (50 Gt / 7 bn), the IRP proposes 6–8 t by 2050, and Bringezu proposes 5 (3–6) t. With 9.66 bn people, a 50 Gt cap gives 5.2 t. **A ~2.7× cut** from today's average is needed. This is the hardest constraint after carbon.

## 5. Good life within boundaries
O'Neill et al. 2018: "no country meets basic needs ... at a globally sustainable level of resource use". Physical needs could likely be met for all, but qualitative goals would take 2–6× the sustainable level at current efficiencies. Fanning et al. 2022: the share of countries overshooting boundaries rose from 32–55% to 50–66% between 1992 and 2015, and business as usual to 2050 deepens overshoot.

## Key verdict
40 GJ final for everyone (386 EJ in 2050) sits **inside the 1.5C-pathway range**: above NZE, below the AR6 C1 median. It **fits comfortably under 2C**. The conditions are global final energy falling 13% below today, electrification to about 50%, and solar plus wind additions of about 1.1–1.3 TW/yr by 2030 (1.7–1.9× the 2025 record), with fossil CO2 near zero by 2050. Because the 1.5C budget is almost spent, "1.5C" now means a limited overshoot with some CDR, whatever the energy level. Energy is not the binding limit for a comfortable life. Carbon timing, diet composition and material throughput are.
