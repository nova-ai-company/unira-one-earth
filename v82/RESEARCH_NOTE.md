# UNIRA V8.2 — completing the economy: the eight missing pieces

After V8.1, an honest review found UNIRA strong on physical feasibility, fairness and governance, but only about 40% complete as an economy that runs day to day. The author asked for all eight gaps to be addressed (2026-11-04), plus a check of real businesses.

| # | Gap | Result | Constitution | Folder |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What to produce without price signals | Orders, waiting lists, monthly forecasts and error-sized stocks serve 96% of wishes within 3 months (3.8% lost, 2.8% waste). The market prices out 19% of wishes, 98% of them in the poorer half; a Soviet-style plan loses 47%. | Art. 10b | `production/` |
| 2 | Hard and unwanted jobs | Hardship steps, shorter hours and work made less hard cut unwilling workers in hard jobs from 87% to 46%. | Art. 14a | `jobs/` |
| 3 | Investment and machines | Maintenance first (83% of investment replaces wear); hours-saving machines bring the week to 27–28 h in 20 years. | Art. 16a | `capital/` |
| + | Restaurant and supermarket | With real cost structures, both pay inputs, rent, energy, wear and UNIRA salaries, and return a community share of 24% and 36% of sales. They break even at 63% and 54% of normal demand. | Art. 16.4–16.5 | `enterprise/` |
| 4 | World money | Equal salaries on day one would ask for twice what exists. Under the author's option C (needs in kind from year 1; salaries converge, target 20 years) with a capacity rule, there are no shortages, and the full scale is reached in 31–39 years with strong catch-up. | T-8 | `worldmoney/` |
| 5 | Trade with non-members | One Trade Office; essential imports first; France's exports cover its essential imports 3.9 times; a six-month reserve. | Art. 12b | `trade/` |
| 6 | Second-hand goods | A public platform; prices capped by wear; no resale of allocated goods within a year. | Art. 18b | – |
| 7 | Sizes | Energy allowance about 5,000 kWh/person/yr, falling toward 3,000; 3 months of essential goods in reserve; pension 2,500–3,750 U plus the retirement account. | Art. 13.6, 11.4, 14b | `sizes/` |
| 8 | Real behaviour | A register of 12 behavioural assumptions, each tied to a pilot measure. | Art. 3a | `BEHAVIOUR_REGISTER.md` |

**What remains uncertain:**
- Most behavioural parameters are still synthetic (see the register).
- The ecological feasibility of the full salary scale for 9–10 billion people requires a lighter consumption basket than France's today.
- The 20-year convergence target needs catch-up growth faster than any large region except China has sustained.
