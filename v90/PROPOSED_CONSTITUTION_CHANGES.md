# V9.0 — proposed constitution changes

**Status (2026-11-04): all three approved by the author and incorporated, with the author's additions.** See `CONSTITUTION_CONSISTENCY_CHECK.md` for the final texts and the consistency check:
- Proposal 1 became **Article 10f — Peaceful delivery and conflict prevention**, extended at the author's request from avoiding harm to active prevention (mediation, early warning, peaceful dispute resolution, cooperation between communities), with independent supervision and a ban on surveillance or repression. Essential services are never withheld because a region is considered unstable.
- Proposal 2 became **Article 10e.6** (monitoring of conflict effects), with independent evaluation, community feedback and protection of sensitive information.
- Proposal 3 became **Article 5.6** in the author's own wording.

The original proposals, as first presented, follow for the record.

## Proposal 1 — Conflict-sensitive delivery (new Article 10f, after Article 10e)

**Finding.** Development programmes can provoke violence while they are being set up: insurgents attack projects that would strengthen the institutions they fight. One programme in the Philippines saw insurgent attacks rise by 110–185% where projects started (Crost, Felter & Johnston 2014, regression-discontinuity design). In the V9.0 model, this backlash matters little worldwide. It does matter in the countries where it occurs.

**Proposed text.**
> **Article 10f — Delivery that does not cause conflict.**
> 1. In regions in conflict or institutional fragility, essential services are delivered in a way that does not give any armed group or faction a reason or an opportunity to attack them. The services are planned with the communities that receive them, and run by them where possible. They are delivered equally to every group living in the area, without favouring any group, faction or region, and their allocation is published.
> 2. Before a large programme starts in such a region, an independent assessment estimates its conflict risk. The programme is adjusted if the assessment finds a serious risk.
> 3. Attacks on essential services are recorded and published, and the Peace Force (Articles 5a–5c) protects water, health, food and energy sites only when the local community asks for it.

**Why each element.**
- *Equally to every group:* inequality between groups is linked to ethnic civil war (Cederman, Weidmann & Gleditsch 2011).
- *Planned with communities:* local participation is the safeguard that lowers backlash in scenarios B–D. The size of that reduction is an assumption.
- *Protection only on request:* this keeps the Peace Force from being seen as an occupying force.

## Proposal 2 — Independent conflict monitoring (addition to Article 10e, point on monitoring)

**Finding.** The decisive unknown is how much of the link between poverty and conflict is causal. In the model that is the fraction `c`, between 0 and 1. It cannot be settled in advance. It can be learned during implementation.

**Proposed text** (new point in Article 10e):
> The independent monitoring body also records, every year and in each region, armed-conflict events, deaths, displacement and attacks on services, using open international data. It publishes an assessment of whether better living standards are being followed by less violence. Programmes are adjusted to what is observed, not to what was expected.

## Proposal 3 — Wording safeguard: peace is not promised

**Finding.** Even the strongest economic scenario (C) leaves most of the remaining conflict risk unrelated to living standards: territory, identity, exclusion from power, state weakness. Only scenario D, which adds hypothetical diplomacy and reconciliation, comes close to ending conflict, and D is an upper bound.

**Proposed text** (in the Preamble's notes or Part I):
> Fair distribution removes some causes of armed conflict, but not all of them. One Earth therefore works for peace by political means as well: mediation, reconciliation, the protection of minorities, and power shared between groups. One Earth does not claim that prosperity alone will bring peace.

## Not proposed

- **No change to Article 10c** (the two living-standard levels) or to Article 10e's targets. V9.0 confirms that conflict delays them; it does not justify lowering them.
- **No "peace dividend" article.** Resources freed by fewer conflicts are already counted through faster delivery. Military spending is already handled by Article 5a.
