# V9.0 — consistency check of the three approved constitutional changes

**Decision (author, 2026-11-04):** the three V9.0 proposals are approved. Article 10f was extended from avoiding harm during delivery to active conflict prevention. The changes were checked against Articles 10c and 10e and against the existing protections before publication.

## The changes, as incorporated in `v75/constitution/UNIRA_CONSTITUTION_DRAFT.md`

- **Article 5.6 — Prosperity does not guarantee peace.** This is the author's wording, unchanged. Only a cross-reference "(Article 10f)" was added at the end.
- **Article 10e.6 — Monitoring of conflict effects.** It extends the independent monitoring body of 10e.4 to assess whether programmes reduce or increase conflict risks. It requires:
  - separating observed patterns from shown causes;
  - independent evaluation;
  - community feedback;
  - protection of sensitive information;
  - yearly publication and adjustment.
- **Article 10f — Peaceful delivery and conflict prevention.** Five points:
  1. delivery that does not increase tensions: fair allocation, local participation, protection against discrimination, independent grievance mechanisms;
  2. services never withheld because a region is considered unstable;
  3. assessing the conflict risks of large programmes;
  4. conflict prevention: mediation, early warning, peaceful dispute resolution, cooperation between communities, with a civilian One Earth Mediation Service;
  5. independent supervision and no surveillance or repression.

## Check against Article 10c (the living standard)

| Requirement of 10c | How the new texts relate | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1: "No one may lack these" | 10f.2: services are never withheld, reduced or delayed because a region is considered unstable. Only an actual lack of safe access may delay delivery, and never anyone's entitlement. | Consistent; strengthens 10c in conflict areas |
| Level 1 priority when goods are short | Not affected. 10f changes how services are delivered, not the priority order. | Consistent |
| Measured by real access | 10e.6 adds conflict effects to the same household-level monitoring. | Consistent |

## Check against Article 10e (essential infrastructure)

| Point of 10e | Relation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 10e.3 "Priority according to need, taking practical delivery into account" | Without a clarification, "practical delivery" could be read as allowing delay for regions judged risky. 10f.2 now separates the two: a physical lack of safe access may delay delivery; a political judgement never may. | Consistent; **clarifies a possible ambiguity** |
| 10e.3 Local participation; security and human rights | 10f.1 and 10f.4 build on them and refer to them. | Consistent |
| 10e.4 Independent monitoring before implementation | 10e.6 uses the same body, so no new monitoring institution is created. | Consistent |
| 10e.5 Targets set by law | Unchanged. V9.0 gives no reason to change the targets. | Consistent |
| Evaluation of programmes | 10e.6: comparisons never delay or withhold a service for the sake of evaluation; delivery order always follows need. Some causal designs, such as random withholding, are therefore excluded. | Consistent (a deliberate limit on research methods) |

## Check against existing protections

| Protection | Risk from the new texts | How it is handled | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Art. 4 and 31: subsidiarity, regional self-government | One Earth intervening in local disputes | Mediation is offered and freely accepted, led by regions and communities where they are able. The Mediation Service only supports. | Consistent |
| Art. 4a.3: minorities | — | 10f.1 protects every group by name. | Strengthened |
| Art. 5.4: the Court settles disputes between regions | Mediation competing with the Court | Disputes between regions still go to the Court. Mediation is offered alongside it. | Consistent |
| Art. 5a–5c: Peace Force, deployment rules | "Protection of services" used to justify deployments | Any use of the Force stays governed only by 5a–5c. Armed protection is never a condition of delivery. Prevention data are never used for Force operations against peaceful people. | Consistent |
| Art. 5b.6: no other armed body | The Mediation Service becoming an armed or intelligence body | It is unarmed and civilian, and keeps no files on persons. | Consistent |
| Art. 6 and 6a: privacy, expression, association, peaceful protest; no censorship | Early warning turning into political surveillance | Early warning uses only open, aggregated information about events and places. It never records opinions, beliefs, associations or peaceful protest, and is never used for policing, prosecution or sanctions. | Consistent |
| Art. 3c.4: no model scores persons | Early-warning models ranking communities | Any model is published and checked under 3c, and scoring of persons **or communities** is forbidden. | Consistent; strengthened |
| Art. 12a.7 and 12a.9: aggregated data only; no scoring from payment data | Use of payment data | 10e.6 and 10f.5 cite 12a. Payment data are not a source. | Consistent |

| Art. 35: complaints answered within 90 days; whistleblowers protected | — | 10f.1 and 10f.5 apply them to grievances and to people reporting tensions. | Consistent |
| Art. 9a: emergencies | Prevention used as a pretext for emergency powers | 10f adds no emergency power. The limits of 9a.5 apply in full. | Consistent |
| Art. 42a: protected core | — | Nothing in the core is weakened. The new texts strengthen Articles 6 and 9 in practice. | Consistent |

## Open points (left to law and research, not decided in the text)

- **How the Mediation Service is appointed, and its size.** Under Article 22a.4, it must not be chosen by a body it supervises or one that supervises it. To be set by law.
- **What "large programme" means in 10f.3,** and the method of the conflict-risk assessment.
- **How effective mediation and early warning are at world scale:** not yet established. This is the next research priority set by the author: preventing conflict through fair institutions, representation, transparent allocation and peaceful dispute resolution.

**Conclusion:** no inconsistency was found with Articles 10c and 10e or with the protected core. One possible ambiguity in Article 10e.3 ("taking practical delivery into account") is resolved by Article 10f.2.
