# UNIRA V7.1 — Does security reduce over-consumption? (rule 20)

**Hypothesis (author):** when people can always afford and access food and essentials, they stop over-buying, hoarding and over-consuming. Analogy: unlimited mobile calls did not lead people to call all day.

**Evidence** (`evidence/`, verified unless noted). **Verdict: true for goods with natural satiation; false for goods without it.**

| good | evidence | supports? |
|---|---|---|
| mobile calls | France: 2h15 → 2h51 a month after unlimited plans (2011–13), then flat (3h03 in 2015, ~7 min/day in 2023) | yes, after an initial rise |
| flat-rate choice | up to 46% of customers pay more for a flat rate than pay-per-use would cost: peace of mind, not maximum use | yes |
| hoarding | Ireland, March 2020: groceries +23%, toilet roll +181%. Scarcity drives stockpiling | yes |
| alcohol and tobacco | cash transfers: no increase (44 estimates, 19 studies) | yes |
| household food waste | differs by only ~7 kg per person per year across income groups | weak |
| mobile data | 0.5 → 14 GB a month (2015–23); unlimited plans +20% use | no (no satiation) |
| energy and travel | rebound <30%; flat-rate heat ~30% more than metered; free transit +14% (Tallinn) to +70% (Dunkirk) trips | no (no satiation) |

**Model effects:**
- **Food:** a security-driven cut in household waste (0 / 5 / 15%) moves comfortable-diet sufficiency only from 1.105 to 1.11–1.12×. Halving waste (rule 13) remains the main lever.
- **Reserves:** security avoids an estimated 25–75% of panic-buying spikes. Catastrophe reserves (rule 13) can be sized for supply shocks rather than demand panics.
- **Energy:** a 20–30% rebound on rich-region savings raises peak world energy to 424–436 EJ, above today's 409 EJ and outside the 1.5 °C envelope. With 10% rebound: 413 EJ.

**Design rule proposed:** goods without natural satiation (energy, travel, data, space) stay **metered and priced per unit** (UNIRA's fixed per-unit prices), never flat-rate or unlimited. Goods with satiation (food, basic goods, calls) can be offered with secure, guaranteed access.
